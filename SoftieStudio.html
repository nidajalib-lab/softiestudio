🌸 Our Story

Spreading Softness & Comfort

Softie Studio started as a tiny passion project in a small cozy room filled with colorful threads and soft fabrics.

How It All Began

In a world that moves so fast, we wanted to create something warm, gentle, and grounding. Softie Studio was born from the simple idea that everyone—no matter their age—deserves a comforting friend to squeeze after a long day.

Every plushie is designed with whimsical love, super-soft materials, and safety in mind. Whether you're decorating your cozy desk or gifting a loved one, our softies are made to spread smiles.
